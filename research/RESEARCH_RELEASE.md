# Nexus Research Release 1.0

Model: Nexus v2.4 Uncrashable/Resilient
Date: 2026-08-26

This release contains 5 explicit hypotheses, 500 paired control/treatment experiments (1,000 simulation runs), 160 ablation runs, 40 sensitivity runs, 30 baseline runs, and 40 pandemic-validation runs: 1,250 seeded simulation runs total.

Core runs use 30 citizens, 6 companies and 360 ticks (15 simulated days) in deterministic/mock cognition mode. Results are evidence about the encoded synthetic model, not empirical causal estimates or forecasts.

Key findings: monetary tightening strongly lowers housing prices but does not move unemployment in the tested configuration; UBI lowers poverty and slightly raises happiness; automation does not generate the expected unemployment response; bank failure strongly lowers the stock-market index but does not lower household wealth; technology raises GDP without changing the explicit productivity metric.

Historical validation is directional only and currently fails a strong pandemic benchmark because the model does not reliably reproduce the joint GDP/unemployment response.

Automated clean-room reproduction passed on five seeds. Independent human replication remains open; see REPLICATION_REQUEST.md.
