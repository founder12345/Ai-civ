# Historical data sources

The historical comparison is directional only.

- U.S. real GDP growth: U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis, National Income and Product Accounts, Table 1.7.1 / 2025 annual update.
- U.S. unemployment: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics annual averages; FRED series UNRATE (BLS source).
- U.S. CPI inflation: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, CPI-U annual average percent change.

The exact reference values used in the release are stored in the downloadable research artifact as `historical_us_reference.json`.
