# Nexus — Public Showcase

## One-line description

**Nexus is a reproducible synthetic civilization laboratory where autonomous agents, markets, institutions and crises interact.**

## Why it is interesting

Nexus connects agent-level decisions to system-level outcomes. Citizens have bounded perception, needs, goals, memory and cognition; they move through a living city and participate in employment, consumption, saving, investment and social networks. Those micro-decisions feed into markets and institutions, while macro conditions feed back into individual behavior.

## Research, not just a demo

The project is structured around explicit hypotheses, seeded experiments, ablations, sensitivity analysis, historical reference comparisons and reproducibility. Failed hypotheses are retained as findings rather than removed from the record.

## Current model

**v2.5 Research Hardened**

- Autonomous citizen cognition
- Need-driven commuting and pathfinding
- USD economy
- Financial markets and banking
- Politics and institutions
- Information and culture
- Cybersecurity
- LLM-assisted cognition with deterministic fallback
- Checkpoint/recovery supervisor
- Experiment and reproducibility tooling
- Living-city visualization

## Scientific position

Nexus is **not** presented as proof of AGI or consciousness, and it is not currently claimed to be a validated real-world forecasting model. Its purpose is controlled experimentation on complex adaptive systems.
